Saleh has structured deals for companies generating millions of dollars in annual revenue, built private equity and venture debt frameworks for HNIs and family offices, and spent years building the infrastructure for Bangladesh's venture investing ecosystem. Bangladesh has roughly 200 active venture investors across private markets today. Meanwhile thousands of companies generate quality revenue and still can't raise growth capital. Most never get funded. Their founders burn their savings, or quietly take a job at a bank instead. The company that could have hired fifty people never gets built.

If Bangladesh could have Ten thousand educated venture investors that will change the entire equation. It means capital chasing good companies instead of sitting idle in a fixed deposit. I didn't build this newsletter to sell information. I built it because every year Bangladesh remains with 200 venture investors in private markets is a year good companies don't get built and good jobs don't get created. You becoming investor number 201, or 2,000, or 8,000 is the actual point.
